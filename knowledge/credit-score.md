# Credit score (Canada)

How to build and protect your score. Data: `data/credit_score.toml`.

Last checked: 2026-10-18

## What matters most

1. **Payment history**: never miss a minimum. Set autopay for at least the minimum.
2. **Utilization**: how much of your limit you use.
3. **Account age**: keep old accounts open.
4. **New credit**: few applications.
5. **Mix**: cards plus a loan helps a little. Do not borrow just for this.

## Utilization targets

- Under **30%**: FCAC's healthy target.
- Under **10%**: top tier.
- **1–4%** on at least one card: often the best score. Not 0% on everything.
- Card companies report the **statement balance**, not the balance on the due date.
- Pay down the balance **before the statement closes** to lower the reported number. Then pay the rest by the due date.

## Old accounts

- Want to drop an annual fee? Ask for a **product change** to a no-fee card.
- Closing your oldest card can shorten your history and raise utilization.
- Use each card a little every few months so it stays open.

## Hard inquiries and mortgages

- Each credit application is a hard check. Checking your own score is not.
- Avoid new credit for **6–12 months** before a mortgage.
- Mortgage prep:
  - 12 months out: pull both reports, fix errors, stop new applications.
  - 6 months out: keep utilization under 10%, no late payments.
  - 1–3 months out: no new credit or big purchases on credit.

## Free access in Canada

- Equifax and TransUnion give free reports online.
- Equifax score: free across Canada. TransUnion score: free mainly in Ontario and Quebec.
- Borrowell (Equifax), free TransUnion score sites and many bank apps show free scores.
- Check **both** bureaus once a year. Dispute errors with the bureau that shows them.

## Rent reporting

- Services like Chexy Credit Builder, FrontLobby or Landlord Credit Bureau can report rent to a bureau.
- Most useful for thin files: newcomers and young adults.

## How long things last

- Scores update at least monthly.
- A late payment stays on your report for about 6 years.
