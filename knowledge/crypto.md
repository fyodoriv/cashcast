# Crypto for Canadians

Where to buy, what it costs, and how it is taxed. Data: `data/crypto_exchanges.toml`.
This is education, not advice. Crypto is high risk; size it so a total loss is survivable.

Last checked: 2026-10-18

## Use a registered platform

- Use platforms registered with Canadian securities regulators (restricted dealer or investment dealer).
- Registered on the checked list: Kraken, Wealthsimple, Newton, Shakepay, Coinbase, Bitbuy and Coinsquare (same company), NDAX, Netcoins, Crypto.com.
- VirgoCX was **not** on the checked list. Confirm its status before using it.
- Check the CSA list before sending money. Status changes.

## Costs

- Compare the **all-in** cost: trading fee + spread + funding and withdrawal fees.
- Examples:
  - NDAX: flat 0.20% per trade; e-Transfer withdrawal 1.50.
  - Kraken Pro: 0.40% maker / 0.80% taker at the lowest tier; instant buy 1% plus spread.
  - Wealthsimple: 0.5% fee plus a spread; no spread on limit orders.
  - Newton: about 1.0–1.6% all-in depending on the coin.
- "Commission-free" apps still charge through the spread.

## Funding

- Interac e-Transfer deposits are usually free.
- Many platforms cap e-Transfers at about 10,000 per transaction. Wires suit large amounts.
- Credit-card purchases of crypto are often cash advances: high fees and instant interest.

## Tax

- Most investors report gains as **capital gains** (50% taxable).
- Frequent trading or running it like a business can make gains **business income** (100% taxable).
- Every sale, crypto-to-crypto swap, or purchase paid in crypto is a disposition. Convert each to CAD.
- Track ACB per coin with the average-cost method.
- The 30-day superficial-loss rule can apply to crypto too.
- Staking rewards are generally income when received.
- Crypto held on a foreign exchange may count toward the 100,000 T1135 threshold. Ask a tax pro.

## Safety

- Turn on 2FA with an authenticator app, not SMS.
- Large long-term holdings: consider a hardware wallet. Back up the seed phrase offline.
- Nobody legitimate asks for your seed phrase.
