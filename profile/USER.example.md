# User profile (example shape)

The structured profile is `private/users/<name>/profile.toml`
(shape: [`profile.example.toml`](profile.example.toml)). This page explains it.

- **Where you live**: `country = "CA"` and your province or territory. Required.
- **Income**: gross yearly employment, self-employment and other income, for tax rates.
- **Room**: RRSP, TFSA and FHSA room from your CRA Notice of Assessment.
- **Goals**: emergency months, risk level, first-time buyer, months to a mortgage, no new credit.
- **Wallet**: the cards you hold (catalog id plus your limit and statement close day).
- **Banking**: your chequing packages and what you keep in each.
- **Rent**: monthly rent and the service you pay it through, if any.
- **Budget**: a monthly amount per category.
- **Watchlist**: tickers to screen; your employer's ticker for the concentration check.
- **Reminders**: the name of your own Reminders list.
